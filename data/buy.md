### Red Wattle Hogs – Pasture-Raised, Heritage Breed

Our Red Wattle hogs are raised outdoors on pasture with room to roam, forage, and grow at a natural pace. The result is richly marbled pork with deep flavor—perfect for families who want to stock their freezer or food lovers who want the very best.

### Our Hog Options

- **Whole Hog (250–300 lbs live weight):** Best for families or groups looking to fill a freezer with a full range of cuts.
- **Feeder Hog (25–75 lbs live weight):** Great for customers who want to raise a hog themselves to market weight.
- **Planning ahead?** Ask about future whole hog or feeder hog availability. The farm must confirm reservation details with you.

### How Buying Works

1. **Send an inquiry.** Use the contact form to tell us which hog option you’re interested in and your preferred timing.
2. **Confirm with the farm.** Availability and reservation details need confirmation; sending the form does not reserve a hog. We sell by pre-order only.
3. **Arrange payment and pickup.** For a whole hog, full payment is required before we deliver it to the local butcher. You arrange cut instructions and pickup directly with the butcher. Feeder hogs are picked up at the farm. We don’t offer shipping or long-distance delivery.
